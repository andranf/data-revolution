Problem
Where we are today
Good procedures,
poor paper trail.
A Python script is generating ~40 SWPs against a Kobelco master template. We have the documents. What we don't have is proof that the right people read them — and a way to catch lapses before WorkSafe does.
Audit risk
No signed record
Nothing ties individual staff to specific SWP versions with a timestamp. An OHS inspector asks "who is competent on chainsaws?" and we can't prove it.
Operational risk
No expiry visibility
Competencies never renew. A greenkeeper who did an induction in 2023 is still "trained" in the filing cabinet, even if they've never touched a verti-drain since.
Adoption risk
Clunky > paper
Tech-literacy varies wildly across 17 staff — full-time, casual, apprentices. If the tool has friction, it'll be worse than the status quo. We have to out-simple the filing cabinet.

Recommendation
The call
Ship a small, purpose-built
web app. Keep the stack behind it.
Notion is our single source of truth for content. Slack is our delivery channel. Zapier is the glue. But the read-and-sign experience — the part staff actually touch — deserves a tiny dedicated interface. That's the whole value.
1 · Library
Notion
Structured DB of every SWP. Authored here, version-controlled, published via API. Andrew and Ben already work here.
2 · Interface
SWP web app
Mobile-first. Renders SWPs, enforces hazard confirmation, captures typed signature + timestamp + IP. Reads/writes to the compliance DB.
3 · Nudge
Slack + Zapier
Scheduled DMs on lead-time + overdue. Block Kit cards deep-link into the app. Supervisor CC at day 3, lockout at day 7.
Why not pure Notion? Notion can hold the data, but it can't gate sign-off behind hazard confirmation, nor capture a tamper-resistant audit record. Forms are ugly. Mobile is clunky. We'd be fighting it.

Why not pure Slack modals? A chainsaw SWP is 4 pages. You can't read that in a modal. Slack is the notification layer, not the document layer.

Risks & trade-offs
Things that could trip us up
What I'm worried about.
High risk
Sign-off theatre
Staff tap through without reading. Hazard checks become muscle memory.
Mitigation
Shuffle hazard order. 2-sec min dwell. Rotating quiz answers. Flag <40-sec sign-offs.
High risk
Contractor gap
Ari isn't on Slack or Workspace. Current design assumes SSO.
Mitigation
Magic-link email sign-off with IP + name capture. Marked 'contractor signoff', valid 48h.
Medium
Version churn
Marcus updates chainsaw SWP — does Tom's sign-off still count?
Mitigation
Material change → re-sign in 14d. Editorial → carry forward. Tick-box on update.
Medium
Reminder fatigue
10 staff × 9 SWPs could mean 2–3 DMs per person weekly. People mute the bot.
Mitigation
Digest mode: one weekly DM summarising due ≤30d. Per-SWP only ≤7d or overdue.
Low
Casual drop-off
Ruby works 2 Saturdays/month. She'll forget the app. Compliance decays invisibly.
Mitigation
SMS fallback via Twilio. Supervisor 'Casual review' view. Re-verify pre-shift.
Low
Scope creep
Leadership sees the dashboard and wants leave, incidents, reviews bolted on.
Mitigation
Hard scope: SWP sign-off only. Incidents stay separate. Say no politely and often.
